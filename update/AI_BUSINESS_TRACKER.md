## CONTINGENCY & PIVOTS

**If Email Sequences Underperform:**
- Shift to blog SEO (organic traffic is more stable)
- Increase lead magnet variety (test 3+ versions)
- A/B test audience targeting (narrow niche vs broad)

**If Blog Takes Too Long:**
- Reduce blog posts to 4 instead of 8 (focus on quality)
- Repurpose YouTube scripts as blog posts
- Partner with guest writers

**If Video Automation Has Quality Issues:**
- Fall back to manual video editing (CapCut + human)
- Use stock footage + voiceover instead of avatar
- Reduce video frequency (1-2/week instead of 4)
