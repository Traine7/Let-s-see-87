# Market Breakdown System

## Use When
You have an idea and need to know if the market is real.

## Prompt
Analyze the market for [NICHE]. Do not give surface insights.

Break it into:
- TAM
- SAM
- SOM
- Top 5 trends shaping demand
- 5 underserved opportunities
- Where money is already flowing

Output as structured insights, not paragraphs.

## Codex Use
Use this before building any major feature in End the Story of π.
