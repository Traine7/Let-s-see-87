# Problem Prioritization Engine

## Use When
You need to decide which problem deserves attention first.

## Prompt
List the top 10 problems in [INDUSTRY].

Rank each by:
- Urgency from 1 to 10
- Willingness to pay from 1 to 10
- Growth speed
- Frequency of complaints
- Current alternatives
- Why existing solutions fail

Output as a ranked table.

## Codex Use
This prevents idea addiction and keeps the Codex attached to real pain.
