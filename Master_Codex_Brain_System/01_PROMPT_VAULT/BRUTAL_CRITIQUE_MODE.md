# Brutal Critique Mode

## Use When
You need to avoid wasting months on the wrong idea.

## Prompt
Act as a brutally honest startup strategist.

Analyze this idea: [IDEA]

Tell me:
- Why it might fail
- What assumptions are weak
- Whether the market already rejected it
- What users actually want instead
- What smaller version should be tested first
- Whether this is a business, feature, content angle, or distraction

Do not flatter me. Help me make a better decision.
