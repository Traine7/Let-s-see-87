# OODA Decision Prompt

## Use When
You need fighter-pilot style decision clarity.

## Prompt
Apply OODA to this decision: [DECISION]

Observe:
- What facts matter?
- What is noise?
- What is uncertain?

Orient:
- What does this mean strategically?
- Who is affected?
- What constraints matter?

Decide:
- What are the top 3 options?
- Which option has the best risk/reward?

Act:
- What is the next irreversible step?
- What is the next reversible experiment?
