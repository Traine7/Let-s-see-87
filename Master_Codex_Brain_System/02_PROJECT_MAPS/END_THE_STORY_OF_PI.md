# End the Story of π

## Concept
A tactical, accessibility-first, AI-guided learning game for veterans, disabled users, overwhelmed adults, and work-from-home parents.

## Core Identity
Duolingo + tactical RPG + AI academy + adaptive command center.

## Aesthetic
Greek neon noir, cyber luxury, scarlet/gold optional, purple/gold primary.

## Pillars
- Adaptive interface
- Tactical mindset
- AI-powered learning
- Voice and text support
- Cognitive support
- Multi-device sync
- Privacy and trust

## First Product Hypothesis
People do not want another course. They want a guided mission system that makes learning feel achievable, personal, and alive.
